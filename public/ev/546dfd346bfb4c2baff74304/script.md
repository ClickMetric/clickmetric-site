## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> First, a customer who searches for a heat pump in Burnaby expects the page they land on to confirm you serve their area — ideally in the headline or the first thing they read. Right now the top of the page doesn't mention Burnaby, BC, or British Columbia, so a careful shopper has to hunt for that reassurance before trusting the offer, and some will simply bounce back to Google. Second, there's no phone number visible on that landing page. For a bigger-ticket decision like a heat pump install, a lot of homeowners want to call and talk to a real person, especially from their phone. If the number isn't right there when they land, you may be quietly losing the callers — the people often most ready to book.
