## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> Your ad leads with 'Open Saturdays & Late Hours: Walk-In Dentist', which is a strong, specific promise for someone searching late at night. When I clicked through, I landed on the homepage, where the first thing a visitor sees is a general welcome rather than a direct confirmation that you're open Saturdays and late evenings.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> First, someone who clicked that ad is paying attention for one thing — are you really open when they need you? Your Saturday nine-to-five and Thursday-until-eight hours are real and genuinely useful, but a visitor has to scroll or click through to the contact page to find them. Putting those hours right at the top of the page they land on would answer their question immediately. Second, the only way to reach you from the top of that page is a phone call. For someone sitting at work or with a child nearby, a short message form next to the phone number would give them a quieter way to get in touch, without losing anyone who prefers to call.

---
