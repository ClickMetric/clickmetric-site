## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> Your ad in the top paid spot reads 'Pest Control Services Vancouver - Vancouver & area'. When someone in Vancouver clicks it, they land on the main Canada-wide homepage rather than a Vancouver-specific page. The headline promises something very local, and the page a visitor arrives at is built to serve every city at once.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> The first thing worth looking at is that routing. Pest Control Services Vancouver already has a Vancouver page with a local 604 phone number and the neighborhoods you cover — West Side, Downtown, North Vancouver, Kitsilano — but paid clicks from a Vancouver search go to the national homepage instead. A Vancouver homeowner has to figure out they are in the right place and self-navigate to find the local information. The second thing is trust. Someone deciding who to let into their home for pest control usually wants to see a star rating, a review count, or a named customer or two near the top of the page. The first screen shows an accreditation badge, which is good, but no visible reviews — and that may be the quietest reason an interested visitor leaves without reaching out.

> Getting clicks is only part of the picture. What matters is how many turn into calls or inquiries, then quotes, and ultimately jobs you actually get paid for. If you can follow that path, you can start seeing which advertising dollars are bringing in real business.
