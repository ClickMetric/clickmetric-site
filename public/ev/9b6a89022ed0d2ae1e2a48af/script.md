## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> The ad for 1 Hour Response Time leads with the headline '1 Hour Response Time | Same Day Appointment Available', which is a sharp, specific promise for someone in a plumbing emergency. When a customer clicks it, though, they land on the general homepage rather than a page built around that one-hour promise.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> The first thing I noticed is that the ad brand says one hour response time, while the matching line on the homepage reads "average 60-minute response time." The number is the same, but one reads like a commitment and the other reads like an average, and for someone standing in a flooded basement that small shift can quietly soften the confidence they felt when they clicked. The second thing is that the big button at the top of the page is "Book An Appointment," which asks the visitor to pick a date and time. Someone with a burst pipe isn't planning anything, they want to call right now, so a large tap-to-call button with your phone number on it would likely match that moment much better.
