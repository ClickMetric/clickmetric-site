## Segment A — Opening (0:00 - 0:12)

> I was searching Google for "best accountant vancouver bc" in Vancouver, and I came across one of your paid ads. I took a closer look at where it sends potential customers, and there are a couple of things worth checking.

## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> Your ad reads 'Vancouver Based CPA Firm | Small Business Accountant', and when someone clicks it, they land on the homepage. The homepage is a general brand overview — it introduces the firm but it doesn't pick up the thread of what the searcher was actually looking for.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> First, when someone searches for an accountant, they're usually trying to decide quickly whether you're the right fit. Right now they land on a page that lists five service areas and leaves them to pick one — pointing the ad to the matching service page would meet that intent sooner. Second, when they do reach the contact form, it asks for nine pieces of information, including a full address and a website, before they've even said hello. Shortening that first step to name, email, phone, and a quick note would make it much easier for a busy owner to get in touch.

---

## Segment D — Business results and measurement (1:00 - 1:18)

> Getting clicks is only part of the picture. What matters is how many turn into calls or inquiries, then quotes, and ultimately jobs you actually get paid for. If you can follow that path, you can start seeing which advertising dollars are bringing in real business.

---

## Segment E — ROI and 15-minute invitation (1:18 - 1:35)

What I've shown you is just a couple of things worth looking at.
