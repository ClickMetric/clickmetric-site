## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> Your ad leads with 'Within 1 Hour | Plumbing Services In Vancouver', which is a strong, specific promise for someone in a hurry. When I clicked it, I landed on the main homepage rather than a page built around that Vancouver search, and the one-hour promise does carry through to the top of the page.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> The first thing a Vancouver customer sees on the page is an Ontario area-code phone number listed first, with the BC number further down, and the page itself describes service across four provinces. For someone who just wants a plumber at their house in the next hour, that mix may make them hesitate about whether you really serve Vancouver. The second thing I noticed is that the "Get an Appointment" button opens a form on another screen rather than showing a short form right in the top of the page. For a customer who would rather type a quick message than call, every extra tap is a chance for them to leave.
