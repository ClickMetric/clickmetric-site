## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> The ad leads with '24-Hour Emergency Service - We are open and ready to serve', which is a strong promise for someone whose furnace just quit on them. When I clicked through, I landed on the main homepage rather than a page built around that emergency promise, so the urgency the ad sets up doesn't fully carry through to what the visitor sees first.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> The first thing I noticed is that the hero at the top of the page welcomes visitors to Chilliwack, even though your ad is reaching people searching in Burnaby. A homeowner in a stressful moment wants to feel like they're in the right place for their city, and a different city name at the top can quietly cause them to bounce back to the search results. The second thing worth mentioning is on the contact page, where the response time is described as "within one business day." That reads very differently from the twenty-four-hour emergency promise in the ad, and it may be worth reconciling those two statements so a late-night caller trusts that someone really is going to pick up.
