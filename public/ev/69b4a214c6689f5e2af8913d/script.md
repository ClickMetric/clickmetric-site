## Segment B — The observed ad and destination (0:12 - 0:25)

> The ad runs under the headline 'Expert plumbers' and clicks through to your main homepage — a clean, professional page, but one that doesn't immediately confirm what an emergency caller needs in that moment.

---

## Segment C — One or two meaningful opportunities (0:25 - 1:00)

> When somebody searches at eleven at night with a burst pipe or a flooded basement, they want to see a line at the top of the page saying we handle emergencies and here's the number to call right now. Your page leads with the brand and shows four side-by-side options — ask a question, schedule a quote, schedule service, leave feedback — and that extra choice can send a stressed homeowner right back to the search results. The second thing worth looking at is call tracking. From the outside, we couldn't see a system that ties phone calls back to the specific ad that produced them, and for a phone-driven business like yours, that's one of the biggest signals you can measure.

---

## Segment D — Business results and measurement (1:00 - 1:18)

> Getting clicks is only part of the picture. What matters is how many turn into calls or inquiries, then quotes, and ultimately jobs you actually get paid for. If you can follow that path, you can start seeing which advertising dollars are bringing in real business.
